Statistical Methods
Volcano Plot — Differential Expression
Statistical test — Three options, selectable in the volcano configuration:
• Welch’s t-test (default): two-sample unequal-variance t-test comparing group means, with Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. P-values from the regularized incomplete beta function. Standard textbook test; appropriate baseline.
• Moderated t-test (limma): empirical-Bayes shrinkage of per-protein variance toward a fitted scaled-inverse-χ2 prior (Smyth 2004). Posterior variance s²post = (d₀·s²0 + dg·s²g) / (d₀ + dg); posterior df = dg + d₀. Hyperparameters d₀ (prior df) and s²0 (prior variance) are estimated from the empirical distribution of per-protein log-variances via method of moments using the digamma/trigamma identities. Recommended for studies with few replicates (≤ 5/group). Ref: Smyth (2004) Stat. Appl. Genet. Mol. Biol. 3:3.
• Peptide-count moderated (DEqMS): extends limma by replacing the single global prior with a per-protein prior that depends on peptide count. Pro-ker’s implementation bins proteins by log₂(peptide count) and uses each bin’s median log-variance as the per-bin prior. Estimates d₀ from the residual log-variance after the binned smoother (so d₀ reflects within-bin variance, not total variance). Falls back to limma if the file has no Peptides column. Ref: Zhu et al. (2020) MCP 19:1047.
Benjamini–Hochberg FDR — Multiple-testing correction controlling the false discovery rate. Adjusted p-values (q-values) are computed by ranking raw p-values and scaling by n/rank. Applied to whichever test was selected above.
S0 correction — A fudge factor added to the t-test denominator (SE + S0) to penalize fold changes driven by low-abundance noise (Tusher et al., 2001). At S0 = 0 this is the standard test. Typical values range from 0.1–2.0. Layered on top of any of the three tests above.
Missing value imputation — Seven methods available, covering the main families established in the proteomics literature (NAguideR, Wang et al. 2020):
• Perseus-style Gaussian (default): draws from a down-shifted normal distribution in log2 space (shift = 1.8 SD, width = 0.3 SD), using the comparison-wide non-zero values to estimate μ/σ. MNAR-aware. Ref: Tyanova et al. (2016) Nature Protocols 11:2301.
• MinProb (canonical): per-sample. Computes MinDet = the smallest observed log2 value in each sample, then for each missing cell draws from N(MinDetsample, σsample) where σ is 1% of the sample's range. MNAR-aware, sample-specific. Ref: Lazar et al. (2016) J. Proteome Res. 15:1116; imputeLCMD::impute.MinProb.
• QRILC: draws from a Gaussian centered at the 1st percentile of the comparison's global log2 distribution. MNAR-aware, single global tail. Ref: Wei et al. (2018) Sci. Rep. 8:663.
• kNN: inverse-distance-weighted mean of the k = 10 nearest proteins (Euclidean distance on shared observed positions). MCAR-aware. Ref: Troyanskaya et al. (2001) Bioinformatics 17:520.
• LLS (Local Least Squares): fits a linear regression of the target protein on its k = 10 nearest similar proteins (in log2 space, with a small ridge for stability), then predicts the missing values. MCAR-aware, often outperforms kNN at high dimensionality. Ref: Kim et al. (2005) Bioinformatics 21:187.
• Random Forest (MissForest): iterative non-linear regression. For each sample column with missing values, trains a random forest on the other samples to predict it (50 trees, depth 6, mtry = √p); iterates with relative-change convergence (tol = 0.01, max 5 iterations). Top-ranked in multiple proteomics benchmarks. Ref: Stekhoven & Bühlmann (2012) Bioinformatics 28:112.
• Min/2: replaces missing values with half the per-protein minimum non-zero value. Simple deterministic baseline; produces "bifurcate" volcano patterns at high missingness, included only as a sanity check.
Reproducibility: All four stochastic methods (Perseus, MinProb, QRILC, MissForest) consume a seed for reproducibility. Per-protein methods (Perseus, MinProb, QRILC) derive a per-protein PRNG seed from the user-supplied Random seed (default 42) and the protein's filtered index: (seed·1000003 + 2·fi + 1or2) mod 232. Matrix-wide MissForest uses the global seed once for bootstrap and feature subsampling across the whole forest. Same seed + same data → identical imputed values and an identical volcano plot. Change the seed to verify a conclusion is robust to the specific draws — a result that depends on a single seed is not robust. The seed is recorded on the Volcano Settings sheet of the XLSX export. kNN, LLS, and Min/2 are deterministic and ignore the seed.
Log2 Fold Change — Calculated as log2(mean_X / mean_Y). Positive values indicate higher abundance in Group X; negative values indicate higher abundance in Group Y.
PCA — Principal Component Analysis
Algorithm — Dual-space PCA (kernel method) optimized for datasets where the number of samples is much smaller than the number of features. Computes the n×n kernel matrix K = X·XT from the mean-centered data matrix, then performs Jacobi eigendecomposition.
Scores — PC scores are eigenvectors scaled by the square root of their eigenvalues. Variance explained is reported as a percentage of total variance for each component.

Statistical Methods Used ▸
Volcano Plot
Test: Three options — Welch’s t-test (default; unequal-variance, Welch–Satterthwaite df), moderated t-test (limma; empirical-Bayes shrinkage of per-protein variance toward a fitted prior, Smyth 2004), or peptide-count moderated (DEqMS; per-bin prior tied to peptide count, Zhu et al. 2020).
Multiple testing: Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction applied to whichever test was selected. Adjusted p-values = raw_p × n / rank.
S0 correction: When enabled, a fudge factor S0 is added to the standard error denominator (SE + S0) to penalize noisy fold changes at low abundance (Tusher et al., 2001; Perseus). Layered on top of any of the three tests.
Imputation: Seven methods spanning every NAguideR family — Perseus-style Gaussian (Tyanova et al. 2016, default), MinProb per-sample MinDet (Lazar et al. 2016), QRILC (Wei et al. 2018), kNN (Troyanskaya et al. 2001), LLS (Kim et al. 2005), Random Forest / MissForest (Stekhoven & Bühlmann 2012), or Min/2 (simple). Stochastic methods (Perseus, MinProb, QRILC, MissForest) use a seeded PRNG — same seed + same data produces identical imputation. Set the seed in the volcano config; the value is recorded in the XLSX Volcano Settings sheet.
Fold change: log2(mean_GroupX / mean_GroupY).
PCA
Method: Dual-space PCA via kernel matrix K = X·XT with Jacobi eigendecomposition. Optimized for n_samples << n_features.
Preprocessing: Column mean centering. No additional scaling.
Output: PC scores (eigenvectors × √eigenvalue). Variance explained as % of total.
Export Data
XLSX puts each analysis (raw file, parsed data, processed data, per-protein log2FC + statistics, PCA scores, volcano settings) on its own sheet. CSV concatenates everything into one file.
